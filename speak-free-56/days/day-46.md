# Day 46 — Divided Attention

**Week 7 · Pressure & Spontaneity · ~21 minutes · Format: walking-and-talking**

## Why today matters

Every recording so far has been made sitting still in a quiet room. Real English never happens there.

It happens while you are walking to a meeting, standing in a noisy corridor, holding something, being looked at. Today you find out how much of your fluency depended on ideal conditions.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Stand up. Describe your day for thirty seconds while walking around the room.
2. Do it again while carrying something in both hands.
3. Say aloud: *"My English has to work while I'm doing something else."*

## The concept (2 min)

Speaking uses working memory. So does walking in a straight line, monitoring traffic, holding a bag, or filtering background noise.

They draw on the same limited pool. Which is why you can speak comfortably at your desk and lose the same sentence in a corridor — nothing about your English changed, only the amount of attention left to spend on it.

The fix is not more vocabulary. It is making the speaking cheaper, and it gets cheaper through practice under load.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Round 1 — Seated baseline (3 min).** Sit still, quiet room. Two minutes on any `medium` prompt. Save as `day-46-seated.m4a`.

**Round 2 — Standing (3 min).** Stand up. New prompt, two minutes. Notice whether your voice changes when your body is not supported. Save as `day-46-standing.m4a`.

**Round 3 — Walking (4 min).** Walk — outside if you can, otherwise around the room. New prompt, two minutes. Save as `day-46-walking.m4a`.

**Round 4 — With noise (3 min).** Play music or a podcast at conversational volume in the background. New prompt, two minutes. Save as `day-46-noise.m4a`.

**The rule: do not slow down to compensate.** The instinct under load is to speak more slowly and carefully. Resist it — you are training the speaking to get cheaper, not the conditions to get easier.

**Compare (1 min).** Play Round 1 and Round 4 back to back. The gap between them is the size of your dependency on ideal conditions.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** all four rounds run alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** walk and talk with someone in a genuinely busy place. Real environments are less forgiving than a podcast playing indoors.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 46 — Distracted Listener** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **Round 4** once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did the load break your speech? |
| **Hesitation** | More searching than when seated? |
| **Clarity** | Did you stay followable with noise present? |

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log the difference between Round 1 and Round 4. A large gap is normal now and should narrow with repetition.

## Stretch (optional)

Speak while doing something with your hands that requires attention — cooking, tidying, assembling something. Manual tasks compete for more attention than walking does.
