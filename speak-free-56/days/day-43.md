# Day 43 — Impromptu

**Week 7 · Pressure & Spontaneity · ~21 minutes · Format: impromptu with 15-second prep**

## Why today matters

Fifteen seconds is not enough to build a speech. That is the point.

It is exactly enough to choose a structure, and today you learn that structure is the only preparation that survives short notice. Content can be found while you are talking. Shape cannot.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud three structures: *past-present-future · problem-cause-solution · for-against-my view*.
2. Pick any object. Take five seconds, then speak about it for thirty using one of those three.
3. Say aloud: *"Fifteen seconds is for choosing a shape, not writing a speech."*

## The concept (2 min)

In fifteen seconds you can do exactly one useful thing: pick a frame.

**Past → present → future** — how it was, how it is, where it goes.
**Problem → cause → solution** — what is wrong, why, what to do.
**For → against → my view** — both sides, then your position.
**Story → point** — one specific example, then what it shows.

Choose one and start. The frame generates the next sentence for you, which means you are never searching for direction — only for words, and words are much easier to find.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Something to listen to:** [video library](../resources/video-library.md#impromptu-and-pressure--days-4348) — take the first clip that fits. Do not browse.

**Round 1 — Fifteen seconds (4 min).** Take a `medium` prompt from [../resources/prompt-bank.md](../resources/prompt-bank.md) without reading it first.

Read it. Start a 15-second timer. Pick a frame — nothing else. Then speak for two minutes, recorded. Save as `day-43-impromptu-1.m4a`.

**Round 2 — Same, harder (4 min).** A `hard` prompt. Fifteen seconds. Two minutes. Save as `day-43-impromptu-2.m4a`.

**Round 3 — Five seconds (4 min).** A `medium` prompt, **five seconds** of preparation, two minutes of speech. Save as `day-43-impromptu-3.m4a`.

**The rules:** the timer is real · no notes, written or mental bullet points · start speaking the instant it ends.

**Compare (1 min).** Play Rounds 1 and 3. Most people find the five-second version *better* — less time to build anxiety, and no plan to lose halfway.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** all three rounds run alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** have them give you the topic and time the fifteen seconds. Not choosing your own topic removes the last hiding place.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 43 — Topic Giver** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **Round 3** once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did two minutes hold with almost no prep? |
| **Recovery** | When you ran out of material, what happened? |
| **Clarity** | Was the frame visible to a listener? |

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log which round was best. If it was Round 3, write down what that tells you about preparation.

## Stretch (optional)

Two minutes on a `hard` prompt with **zero** preparation — start speaking the moment you read it.
