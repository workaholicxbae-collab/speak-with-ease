# Day 53 — Body & Voice

**Week 8 · Own the Room · ~21 minutes · Format: mirror talk**

## Why today matters

Your listener starts forming an impression before you finish your first sentence, and most of it comes from how you are standing.

This is the one day that is not really about English. It is about removing the physical signals that undercut everything you say.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Stand up. Feet apart, weight even on both. Shoulders back and down.
2. Say your hook from this position. Notice the voice change — it comes from the ribcage opening, not from confidence.
3. Say aloud: *"I stand still and I let them look at me."*

## The concept (2 min)

Four things, in order of how much they cost you:

**Posture** — weight on both feet, still. Shifting from foot to foot reads as wanting to leave, and it is the most common nervous tell.
**Volume** — slightly louder than feels natural. Nervous speakers are consistently too quiet, and quiet is read as uncertain regardless of content.
**Hands** — visible, at waist height, moving when you emphasise. Not in pockets, not behind your back, not gripping each other.
**Eyes** — one person, one sentence. Finish a sentence looking at someone, then move. Scanning the room continuously looks like searching for an exit.

None of this needs to be graceful. It only needs to stop contradicting you.

## Today's work (13 min)

You need a mirror or a phone camera. Use the first ninety seconds of your talk throughout.

**Round 1 — Watch (3 min).** Deliver to the mirror. Fix nothing. Note what your hands and feet did unasked.

**Round 2 — Posture and volume (3 min).** Again. Still feet, volume one step up from comfortable. Record audio as `day-53-voice.m4a`.

**Round 3 — Hands (3 min).** Again, hands visible at waist height, moving only on yesterday's stressed words.

**The rule: no pockets, no crossed arms, no gripping.** If unsure, rest them open at waist height.

**Round 4 — Video (4 min).** Record ninety seconds on video, saved beside `day-53-voice.m4a`.

Watch it **[on mute first](../resources/video-library.md#delivery-pausing-and-stage-presence--days-51-52-53).** Muting removes the English and shows only what a listener sees before you have said anything.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** mirror and camera work alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** deliver ninety seconds and ask one question — *"where did I look uncomfortable?"* People notice instantly and rarely volunteer it.

**Upgrade — with AI:** not useful today. This one is visual; use the mirror.

## Record & review (3 min)

Watch the video on mute, then listen to `day-53-voice.m4a`. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Expression** | Did your body match what you were saying? |
| **Clarity** | Was the volume enough to carry? |
| **Flow** | Did managing your body disrupt your speaking? |

Flow usually drops today. Adding a physical task costs speech at first and recovers within a day or two.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log the one physical habit you most want to drop. One only. Trying to fix four things on Day 55 fixes none.

## Stretch (optional)

Deliver the whole talk on video and watch it muted, twice. Painful, and more informative than any amount of listening.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you run the whole thing, start to finish, no stopping. The last rehearsal before it is real.
