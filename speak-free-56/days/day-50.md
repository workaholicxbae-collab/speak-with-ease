# Day 50 — Pick Your Talk

**Week 8 · Own the Room · ~20 minutes · Format: outline and pitch**

## Why today matters

Six days from now you deliver a five-minute talk to a real person. Today you choose what it is about, and the choice matters more than anything you will do to it afterwards.

Pick wrong and you spend a week pushing something uphill. Pick right and delivery gets easier every day.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud three things you could talk about for five minutes with no preparation at all.
2. Say which of the three you would still want to talk about next month.
3. Say aloud: *"Interesting to me beats impressive to them."*

## The concept (2 min)

A good topic passes three tests:

**You have an opinion, not just information.** "How our supply chain works" is a briefing. "Why our supply chain keeps failing in the same place" is a talk. The difference is that one has a position.

**You could answer a question about it without preparing.** If you cannot, you have chosen something you researched rather than something you know.

**You would say it to a friend unprompted.** If the topic only exists because you needed a topic, it will show in every rehearsal this week.

Expertise is not required. Caring is.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Generate five (3 min).** Say five possible topics aloud. Do not evaluate them — just produce them. Force the fifth even if it feels weak.

**Test them (3 min).** For each, say aloud: *"My position on this is…"* Any topic where you cannot finish that sentence is out.

**Choose one (1 min).** The one you spoke about most easily, not the one that sounds most serious.

**Outline (5 min).** Say aloud, never in full sentences:

1. **The claim** — what you want them to believe at the end. One sentence.
2. **Three points** — each supporting it.
3. **The close** — what should change.

Record it as you speak. Save as `day-50-outline.m4a`.

**Pitch it (1 min).** The whole talk in sixty seconds. If you cannot compress it, the claim is not clear yet.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** the whole process runs alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** give them the sixty-second pitch and ask what they think your main point was. If they get it wrong, fix the claim today, not on Day 54.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 50 — Topic Tester** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to the **sixty-second pitch** once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Was the claim stated in one sentence? |
| **Expression** | Did you sound like you cared? |
| **Flow** | Did the outline come out without stalling? |

Expression is the real test today. If you sound flat about your own chosen topic, change the topic now. You have six days left and no time to fall in love with it later.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also write your chosen topic in the tracker. Committing it in writing makes it much harder to quietly swap on Day 53.

## Stretch (optional)

Pitch two of your five topics and compare. Sometimes the second one is obviously better once spoken aloud.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you build the frame. Hook, three points, close — and nothing else is allowed in.
