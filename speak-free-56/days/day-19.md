# Day 19 — Field Mission

**Week 3 · Everyday Conversation · ~20 minutes · Format: live delivery to a human**

## Why today matters

Everything for eighteen days has been rehearsal. Today is the performance, and it is deliberately small: three minutes of English with a real person.

Not a test. Not an important conversation. Three ordinary minutes with someone who has no idea they are part of a program.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say your three openers from Day 15 aloud, twice each.
2. Say aloud: *"If it goes badly, nothing happens."* Say it again, because it is true.
3. Decide **now** who and where. Not "someone today" — a specific person, a specific place, a specific time.

## The concept (2 min)

The fear says the stakes are high. Test the claim.

What is the actual worst outcome? They do not understand you and you repeat yourself. They are busy and the conversation is short. You use a wrong word and they answer anyway.

None of those are damage. In every one, you finish the day having spoken English to a human being — which is the only thing being measured.

The stakes are not high. They have never been high. Your body has just been insisting otherwise.

## Today's work (12 min)

**Choose your target (2 min).** Easiest to hardest — pick one you will actually do:

- A colleague you already speak to
- A shopkeeper or barista
- A neighbour
- A stranger in a queue
- A phone call to any business

**Set the goal (1 min).** Three minutes of English. That is all. Not impressive English. Not error-free English. Three minutes.

**Go (6 min).** Open with a Day 15 opener. Then use Day 17: ask, listen, follow up with a word from their answer.

**The rule: do not explain that you are practising.** The moment you announce it, they adjust their English and you get an easier conversation than the one you needed.

**Debrief immediately (3 min).** Within five minutes of finishing, record 90 seconds on: what you said, what surprised you, what you would do differently. Save as `day-19-debrief.m4a`.

Record it straight away. Memory rewrites these conversations into failures within about an hour.

## Interactive layer

**With a person (default today):** the mission above.

**Solo fallback — genuinely nobody available:** use an AI voice mode for three unbroken minutes, then complete the mission tomorrow. Do not skip it. Note in your tracker that you used the fallback.

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to your debrief once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did the conversation move, or lurch? |
| **Recovery** | When you lost a word, what happened? |
| **Expression** | Did you sound like yourself, or a smaller version? |

Score the conversation as you remember it — the debrief recording is only the record.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log one line: **what you feared, and what actually happened.** Read that line again on Day 55.

## Stretch (optional)

A second conversation today, with someone harder. Or the same person, twice as long.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow is smaller and stranger. You learn to react in one second — the cheapest, most useful move in conversation.
