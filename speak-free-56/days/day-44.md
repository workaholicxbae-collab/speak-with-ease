# Day 44 — Table Topics ×5

**Week 7 · Pressure & Spontaneity · ~20 minutes · Format: rapid-fire topics**

## Why today matters

Yesterday you had fifteen seconds between reading a topic and speaking. Today you have none, five times over.

The exhaustion is the exercise. By topic four you will have no energy left for self-consciousness — and what remains is the version of you that just talks.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud three sentences about the first thing you see. Immediately.
2. Do it again with the second thing. And the third.
3. Say aloud: *"Tired is fine. Stopping is not."*

## The concept (2 min)

Self-consciousness is expensive. Monitoring how you sound, predicting the next word, checking the grammar — all of it runs on the same attention you need for speaking.

Fatigue takes that budget away. When you are five topics deep and out of energy, there is nothing left to spend on watching yourself, so the watching stops.

This is why the last topic is usually the best one. Not because you warmed up, but because you ran out of capacity to interfere.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Something to listen to:** [video library](../resources/video-library.md#impromptu-and-pressure--days-4348) — take the first clip that fits. Do not browse.

**Prepare (1 min).** Take **five** prompts from [../resources/prompt-bank.md](../resources/prompt-bank.md) — mix `medium` and `hard`. Write the numbers only. Do not read them.

**The run (10 min).** Press record once and leave it running for all five.

1. Read topic one aloud. Speak for 90 seconds.
2. At 90 seconds, read topic two aloud and start immediately.
3. Continue through all five. **No breaks. No water. No comments between.**

Save as `day-44-table-topics.m4a`.

**The rules:** no gap longer than two seconds between topics · no restarting · if you dry up, describe why the topic is difficult and keep going — that still counts.

**Review (2 min).** Play topic one and topic five. Note which sounds more relaxed. It is almost always five.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** the run works alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** have them read the topics and keep time. Not knowing what is coming is the realistic version.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 44 — Topic Machine** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **topics 4 and 5** only. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did the late topics move more freely? |
| **Recovery** | When you dried up, did you keep producing sound? |
| **Expression** | Did fatigue flatten you, or relax you? |

Expression splits people here. Some go flat with tiredness; others lose their guard and sound more like themselves than at any point in the program. Find out which you are.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log which topic was your best. If it was four or five, that is the most useful thing you will learn this week.

## Stretch (optional)

Seven topics instead of five, at two minutes each. Fourteen minutes of continuous unprepared speech is genuinely hard and genuinely transformative.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow someone cuts you off mid-sentence — and you continue without going back to the beginning.
