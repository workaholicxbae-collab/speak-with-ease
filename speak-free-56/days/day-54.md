# Day 54 — Full Rehearsal

**Week 8 · Own the Room · ~22 minutes · Format: full rehearsal recording**

## Why today matters

Four days of parts. Today the whole thing runs, start to finish, no stopping.

You will find problems you could not have found any other way — a point that takes twice as long as planned, a transition that does not exist, an ending that arrives before you are ready. Better today than tomorrow.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say your five-part skeleton aloud from memory. Hook, claim, three points, objection, close.
2. If any part is missing, drill it once now.
3. Say aloud: *"If I lose a sentence, I go to the next part. I do not go back."*

## The concept (2 min)

A rehearsal is not a performance with fewer people. It has a different job: finding the breaks.

So do not stop to fix things. Run the whole thing, note where it went wrong, then decide what to change. Stopping to repair each fault means you never learn whether the talk holds together end to end — and holding together is the only thing tomorrow tests.

Every talk has one section that runs long. You will not know which until you run it.

## Today's work (14 min)

**Run 1 — Full, timed (6 min).** Deliver the whole talk. Stand. Record. Time it.

**The rules:** no stopping · no restarting · no notes · if you lose your place, move to the next part of the skeleton and keep going.

Save as `day-54-rehearsal-1.m4a`.

**Diagnose (3 min).** Note three things:

- **Actual length** against your five-minute target
- **Where it ran long** — almost always one of the three points
- **Where you lost your place**, if anywhere

**Fix one thing (2 min).** One only. Usually cutting the longest point down, not adding anything anywhere.

Do not restructure. It is too late for that, and the structure is not the problem.

**Run 2 — Full, again (3 min).** Deliver the whole talk once more with the fix in. Save as `day-54-rehearsal-2.m4a`.

**Do not do a third run.** Two is the useful number. Beyond that you begin memorising sentences, and a memorised talk breaks the moment one line goes missing.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** both runs alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** deliver Run 2 to someone and ask one question afterwards — *"what was my main point?"* If they cannot say it back, your claim is not landing, and that is fixable tonight.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 54 — Rehearsal Audience** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **Run 2** once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did five minutes hold without collapse? |
| **Clarity** | Were the three points distinguishable from each other? |
| **Recovery** | When you lost your place, did you continue? |

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log **who you are delivering to tomorrow, and when.** Write the name. A named person and a time is the difference between doing Day 55 and quietly skipping it.

## Stretch (optional)

Run it once more tomorrow morning, out loud, before the real delivery. A morning run-through settles the structure without over-rehearsing it.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow it is real. One person, minimum, looking at you while you speak.
