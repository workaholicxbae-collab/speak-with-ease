# Day 7 — Replay

**Week 1 · Break the Silence · ~12 minutes · Format: reflection and comparison**

## Why today matters

Six days ago you recorded ninety seconds and refused to listen to it. Today you listen.

This is a light day on purpose. You have spoken every day for a week; the muscle needs a quieter session. But quiet is not empty — today produces the first piece of evidence in the program, and evidence is what keeps you here in Week 4 when motivation is gone.

## Warm-up (2 min)

No vocal warm-up today. Instead:

Before you press play, write down in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md) what you expect to hear. One line. Be honest, including the unkind version.

Then find what you wrote on Day 1 under *"What I expect to hear when I play this back on Day 7."*

## The concept (2 min)

You are a terrible judge of your own speaking, and you are biased in one specific direction: you remember the moments you froze and you delete the moments you flowed.

That is not modesty. It is how memory works under threat — the failures get encoded, the ordinary parts do not.

The recording does not have that bias. This is why the file exists.

## Today's work (6 min)

**Listen (2 min).** Play `day-01-baseline.m4a` from beginning to end. Once. Do not skip. Do not stop it early because you dislike your voice.

**Write three wins (2 min).** Three specific things that were **better than you remembered**. Not "it was okay" — specific: *"I got through the middle without stopping."* · *"My voice was steadier than expected."*

Three, not two. If you are struggling, listen again. They are there.

**Write one focus (1 min).** One thing for Week 2, phrased as an action rather than a complaint. Not *"too many ums"* but **"pause silently instead of filling"**.

**Average the week (1 min).** Add your seven checkpoint scores, divide by seven, and write it as **Week 1 average**. That number is compared on Days 14, 28, 42 and 56.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** today is solo by design. This one is between you and the recording.

**Optional — with a person:** play them Day 1 and ask what they actually hear. Outside listeners are consistently kinder and more accurate than you are. Only do this with someone safe.

## Record & review (2 min)

No recording today.

Instead, score the **Day 1 baseline** — the file, not your memory of it — on these three:

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did it move? |
| **Hesitation** | How much searching? |
| **Recovery** | What happened after a stumble? |

Write the three scores next to Day 1 in your tracker. On Day 56 you score that same file again, and the difference between today's scores and Day 56's tells you how much your *ear* changed — which improves before your speech does.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?**

There was no speaking task, so rate the shorter version instead: *how much did you hesitate before pressing play?* That number is about fear, not fluency, and it is worth having on record.

## Stretch (optional)

Complete the **weekly challenge** if you have not yet: speak English out loud to one human being who does not live with you. You have until tonight. Thirty seconds counts.

## Tomorrow

Week 2 starts. We go after the two-second delay — the pause where you translate before speaking. By Day 14 you re-record today's baseline prompt and hear the difference directly.
