# Day 28 — Portfolio Piece

**Week 4 · Storytelling & Personality · ~12 minutes · Format: reflection and comparison**

## Why today matters

Halfway. Four weeks done, four to go.

Today you record one clean piece — your best story, told properly — and then play the Day 1 baseline for the third time. This is the biggest gap you will hear until Day 56.

## Warm-up (2 min)

No vocal warm-up. Instead:

Write one line in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md): **what you can do now that you could not do on Day 1.** One specific thing, not "I'm better".

Write it before you listen to anything, so the recording cannot talk you out of it.

## The concept (2 min)

Everything so far has been drills, and drills are not evidence. Today you produce a finished thing.

A portfolio piece is a single recording you would be willing to let someone hear — not perfect, but complete and deliberate. It matters because it is the first time in four weeks the target has been *quality* rather than *completion*.

You get one more of these, on Day 56.

## Today's work (6 min)

**Choose and prepare (1 min).** Your strongest story from this week. Ninety seconds. Everything you have built goes in: spine, chosen length, timing, sensory detail.

**Record (2 min).** One take. Save as `day-28-portfolio.m4a`.

**The rule: three takes maximum, and you keep the third whatever happens.** This is a portfolio piece, so polishing is allowed — but not endless polishing, which is just avoidance with better manners.

**Replay Day 1 (2 min).** Play `day-28-portfolio.m4a`. Then play `day-01-baseline.m4a`.

New first, old second. Always.

**Write three differences (1 min).** Specific, not general:

- *"Day 1 I stopped four times. Today, none."*
- *"Day 1 I never varied my voice. Today the ending is quieter than the middle."*

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** today is solo.

**Optional — with a person:** play them the portfolio piece with no explanation. Ask what they think of the story, not of your English. If they respond to the story, that is the result.

## Record & review (2 min)

Score the **portfolio piece** on these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Expression** | Would a stranger want to keep listening? |
| **Clarity** | Did the story land without effort? |
| **Flow** | Did it move all the way through? |

**Average the week (1 min).** Add Days 22–28, divide by seven, log as **Week 4 average**.

Then look at all four weekly averages together. Four points is a trend. If it is flat, check whether you have been taking the easy prompt every time.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it.

## Stretch (optional)

Send the portfolio piece to the person from Day 5. They heard you four weeks ago. Ask them directly whether anything sounds different.

## Tomorrow

Week 5, and the work changes. Until now, being wrong was safe because nothing was at stake. From tomorrow you take positions, and someone argues back.
