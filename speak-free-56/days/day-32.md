# Day 32 — Polite Pushback

**Week 5 · Opinions & Disagreement · ~22 minutes · Format: scenario roleplay**

## Why today matters

You disagree with someone. What comes out is: *"Sorry — no, sorry, I mean, maybe I'm wrong, but I was just thinking, possibly…"*

By the time the disagreement arrives, it has been apologised into nothing. Today you put the disagreement first and the softening second, which is the whole technique.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud: *"I don't agree with that."* Flat, no smile in the voice, no apology.
2. Say it three more times until it stops feeling rude. It is not rude.
3. Say aloud: *"Disagreeing is not an attack. It is a contribution."*

## The concept (2 min)

Politeness lives in **structure**, not in apology.

The reliable pattern is three moves:

1. **Acknowledge** — *"I see what you mean about the cost."*
2. **Disagree clearly** — *"I don't think that's the main problem, though."*
3. **Offer** — *"What if we looked at the timeline instead?"*

Move one shows you listened. Move two is unambiguous. Move three keeps it collaborative.

Notice what is absent: any apology for having a view. "Sorry" belongs to things you did wrong, and disagreeing is not one of them.

## Today's work (14 min)

**Something to listen to:** [video library](../resources/video-library.md#opinions-and-disagreement--days-2934) — take the first clip that fits. Do not browse.

Four scenes. Both roles, recorded. Use the three-move pattern every time.

**Scene 1 — A friend (3 min).** They suggest a plan you do not want. Push back and propose something else. Save as `day-32-friend.m4a`.

**Scene 2 — A colleague (3 min).** They propose an approach you think is wrong. Disagree on the substance, not the person. Save as `day-32-colleague.m4a`.

**Scene 3 — Someone senior (4 min).** Your manager suggests something you believe will not work. Disagree upward, clearly, without shrinking. Save as `day-32-senior.m4a`.

**Scene 4 — Someone who pushes back (4 min).** They disagree with your disagreement. Hold your position through one more exchange. Save as `day-32-pushback.m4a`.

**The rule: never say "sorry" before disagreeing.** Not once, in any scene. If it slips out, keep going and note it.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** all four scenes run alone, both roles.

**Upgrade — with a person:** ask them to propose something and genuinely defend it. Real resistance is much harder than the version you write for yourself.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 32 — Pushback Partner** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **Scene 3** only. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Was your disagreement unmistakable? |
| **Expression** | Did you sound steady, or apologetic? |
| **Recovery** | When challenged, did you hold or fold? |

Scene 3 is chosen deliberately. Disagreeing upward is where most people collapse, and it is the situation that matters most at work.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log how many times "sorry" escaped across the four scenes.

## Stretch (optional)

Run Scene 3 again with the senior person becoming irritated. You are training the ability to stay level when the temperature rises.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow the pushback does not stop after one round. Five exchanges, and no retreating.
