# Day 48 — The Gauntlet

**Week 7 · Pressure & Spontaneity · ~22 minutes · Format: adversarial Q&A**

## Why today matters

Ten hard questions, one after another, with no escape answers permitted.

You have handled questions before, but always with a way out — "it depends", "I'd have to check", a joke. Today those exits are closed, and you find out what you produce when there is nowhere to go.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud: *"That's a fair question."* Three times. This is your legitimate two-second buy.
2. Say a `hard` prompt aloud and answer it in one sentence. Immediately.
3. Say aloud: *"I answer the question I was asked."*

## The concept (2 min)

Under pressure people reach for four escapes. All of them read as evasion.

- *"It depends"* — without naming the condition. Day 34 already closed this one.
- *"That's a good question"* — followed by nothing.
- *"I'd have to check"* — when you could give a partial answer now.
- Answering a different, easier question than the one asked.

The legitimate move is a **buy**: one short phrase that costs two seconds and commits you to answering.

> *"Fair question — give me a second."*
> *"Two things on that."*

The second is the strongest sentence in this entire program. Saying "two things" forces you to produce two things, and the structure appears before you have thought of either.

## Today's work (14 min)

**Something to listen to:** [video library](../resources/video-library.md#impromptu-and-pressure--days-4348) — take the first clip that fits. Do not browse.

**Choose a topic (1 min).** Something you know — your work, your city, your field. You will be attacked on it for ten questions.

**The gauntlet (10 min).** Ten hard follow-up questions, one at a time, recorded continuously. Each question must build on your previous answer.

If working alone, play both roles and make the questioner genuinely difficult. Use *why*, *how do you know*, *what if you're wrong*, *who says*, *what's the cost*.

Save as `day-48-gauntlet.m4a`.

**The rules:** no escape answers · every answer starts within three seconds · a buy phrase is allowed, silence is not · you must answer the question that was actually asked.

**Review (3 min).** Play it back. Mark every escape you used. Most people use two or three without noticing.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** both roles, ten questions.

**Upgrade — with a person:** ask them to be relentless and to follow up on your weakest answer every time.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 48 — Gauntlet Questioner** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **questions 7 to 10** only. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Recovery** | After a question you could not answer, what happened? |
| **Clarity** | Did you answer the actual question? |
| **Expression** | Did you stay level as it got harder? |

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log how many escape answers you used. Compare with Day 41 — this is the same habit under heavier pressure.

## Stretch (optional)

Fifteen questions where the questioner disagrees with everything you say. Sustained disagreement is a different load from sustained difficulty.
