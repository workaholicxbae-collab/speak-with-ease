# Day 55 — Go Live

**Week 8 · Own the Room · ~20 minutes · Format: live delivery to a human**

## Why today matters

Fifty-four days of preparation, and today it is real. One person minimum, in the room or on camera, looking at you while you speak for five minutes.

This is what the program was for. Everything else was scaffolding.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say your skeleton aloud once. Hook, claim, three points, objection, close.
2. Stand. Feet even. Shoulders back. Say your hook once, at volume.
3. Say aloud: *"They want me to do well. Nobody is hoping I fail."*

That last line is true and worth saying out loud. Audiences are on the speaker's side by default — being watched feels like being judged, but it almost never is.

## The concept (2 min)

Three things will happen today, and none of them is a problem.

**You will speak faster than you rehearsed.** Everyone does. Build in one deliberate pause after your claim — it will feel enormous and sound normal.

**You will lose one thing.** A sentence, an example, a whole point. Nobody knows it was supposed to be there. Only you have the script.

**You will finish and not remember most of it.** This is why you record. Your memory of today will be much worse than the recording, and the recording is the honest one.

## Today's work (12 min)

**Set up (2 min).** Your audience — one person or more, in person or on video. **Record the audio.**

**Deliver (5 min).** The whole talk. Standing. No notes.

**The rules:** no apology at the start — no *"sorry, my English isn't great"* · if you lose your place, move to the next part · one deliberate pause after the claim · finish on your close, then stop talking.

Save as `day-55-live.m4a`.

**Ask one question (2 min).** Afterwards, ask your audience exactly one thing:

> *"What do you think my main point was?"*

Not "how was it?" — that produces politeness. The main-point question produces information.

**Debrief (3 min).** Within five minutes, record 90 seconds: what you cut, where they reacted, what you would change. Save as `day-55-debrief.m4a`.

## Interactive layer

**With a person (default today):** as above.

**Solo fallback — genuinely nobody available:** deliver to a video camera, then send the video to one person and ask the main-point question. Weaker, and you should know it is weaker. Do the live version within three days.

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to `day-55-live.m4a` once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Expression** | Did you deliver it, or recite it? |
| **Recovery** | When you lost something, did it show? |
| **Flow** | Did five minutes hold in front of a person? |

Score it against **your Day 1 self**, not against a professional speaker. That is the only comparison this program has ever been making.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log what your audience said your main point was. If they got it right, your structure worked — that is a harder and more useful result than sounding fluent.

## Stretch (optional)

Deliver it a second time to a different person. The second delivery is always better, and knowing that is useful the next time a first attempt disappoints you.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow is the last day. You play Day 1 for the final time, write yourself a letter, and decide what happens next.
