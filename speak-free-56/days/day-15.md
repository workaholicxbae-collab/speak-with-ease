# Day 15 — Openers

**Week 3 · Everyday Conversation · ~20 minutes · Format: drilled repetition**

## Why today matters

The hardest sentence in any conversation is the first one. Once you are two exchanges in, you are fine — it is the opening that you rehearse in your head and then never say.

Today you make eight openers automatic. Not memorised for a test; drilled until they arrive without a search, the way your first language does.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say "hi, how's it going?" out loud five times, faster each time.
2. Say it as though greeting a friend. Then a stranger. Then a manager.
3. Say aloud: *"The first line is not a test. It is a door."*

## The concept (2 min)

Fluent small talk is not invented. It is a small set of reusable phrases that everyone recycles endlessly.

There are maybe fifteen openers in ordinary English, and native speakers use the same ones every day without noticing. There is nothing creative about it. You have been trying to invent something that was always meant to be borrowed.

Take the eight below and stop composing.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Something to listen to:** [video library](../resources/video-library.md#everyday-conversation--days-15-16-17-20) — take the first clip that fits. Do not browse.

**The eight openers (2 min).** Read these aloud once:

1. *How's your day going?*
2. *Busy week?*
3. *Have you been here before?*
4. *What have you got planned this weekend?*
5. *How do you know [person / this place]?*
6. *Sorry, do you know if…?*
7. *That looks good — what is it?*
8. *Long queue today.*

**Drill (6 min).** Say each one **five times aloud**. Forty repetitions total.

Change something every time — faster, slower, warmer, flatter. Identical repetition trains nothing; varied repetition builds a phrase you can actually deploy.

**Record (3 min).** Record yourself saying all eight, once each, as though to a real person. Save as `day-15-openers.m4a`.

**The rule: no gap before any opener.** If you hesitate before one, that one is not ready. Drill it five more times.

**Add two of your own (2 min).** Two openers that fit your actual life — your office, your commute, your shop. Say each five times. Write them in your tracker.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** the drill runs alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** use three of these on someone today. Real deployment beats forty repetitions.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 15 — Opener Partner** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Expression** | Did they sound like greetings, or like a list? |
| **Flow** | Any hesitation before starting one? |
| **Clarity** | Would each land first time? |

Expression is the one that matters. A perfectly pronounced opener delivered flatly does not open anything.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log which of the ten openers still feels unnatural. Drill that one again tomorrow.

## Stretch (optional)

Practise the *second* line — what you say after they answer your opener. That gap is where most conversations die.

## Tomorrow
