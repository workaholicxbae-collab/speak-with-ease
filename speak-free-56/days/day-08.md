# Day 8 — Inner Narration

**Week 2 · Think in English · ~21 minutes · Format: inner narration**

## Why today matters

The two-second delay before you speak does not start in your mouth. It starts in your head, where you are building the sentence somewhere else and shipping it across.

Today you move the building site. You narrate your own life in English, silently first — no pronunciation to worry about, no listener, nothing to be judged on — and only then out loud.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Look around and silently name five things you can see. In English. Do not say them aloud.
2. Now silently narrate what your hands are doing. Ten seconds.
3. Say aloud: *"I am sitting here and I am about to talk about my morning."*

You just thought in English three times. It cost you nothing.

## The concept (2 min)

Your inner voice already runs constantly. It comments on the queue, the weather, the person who did not indicate.

That voice runs in your first language, so it gets thousands of hours of practice every year while your English gets twenty minutes. The imbalance is not talent. It is airtime.

Today you give English some of that airtime. Silent thinking is speaking practice with the fear removed.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Stage 1 — Silent (4 min).** Sit still. Narrate your morning in your head, in English, from waking up to right now. Full sentences, not word lists.

When you hit something you cannot say, do not stop and do not switch languages. Go around it: *"I ate the — the round bread thing."* Then continue.

**Stage 2 — Whispered (3 min).** Same narration, from the beginning, whispering. Whispering keeps the fear low while adding the physical movement.

**Stage 3 — Aloud and recorded (4 min).** Full voice. Record 2 minutes on the same content. Save as `day-08-narration.m4a`.

**The rule: no restarts and no silent gaps over two seconds.** You have now told this story twice. The third telling should move.

**Stage 4 — Notice (2 min).** Play it back. Mark each point where you clearly translated — the giveaway is a pause followed by an unusually formal sentence.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** all four stages run alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** narrate your morning to someone while they do something else. A half-listening audience is the easiest real audience there is.

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did stage 3 move faster than stage 1 felt? |
| **Hesitation** | How many translation pauses did you mark? |
| **Clarity** | Would a stranger follow the order of your morning? |

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log the number of translation pauses you marked. That number is the real measure this week, and it should fall by Day 14.

## Stretch (optional)

Narrate the next thirty minutes of your actual day in English, silently, as it happens. Not a memory — live commentary on your own life.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you start cold. One random word, sixty seconds, no run-up — three times over.
