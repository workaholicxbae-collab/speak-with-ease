# Day 14 — Compare

**Week 2 · Think in English · ~12 minutes · Format: reflection and comparison**

## Why today matters

Two weeks ago you recorded ninety seconds and could barely stand to hear it.

Today you record the same prompt again and play them back to back. This is the first hard measurement in the program — not a feeling, not an impression, but two files and the difference between them.

## Warm-up (2 min)

No vocal warm-up. Instead:

Before you record anything, write one line in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md): **what you expect the difference to be.** Be honest, including if you expect none.

Do not listen to Day 1 yet. Recording first, listening second — otherwise you will imitate the old version.

## The concept (2 min)

Two weeks is not long enough to change your English. It is long enough to change your *access* to the English you already had.

So do not listen for better grammar or new vocabulary. Listen for four things:

- How long before you started talking
- How many times you stopped
- How much filler
- Whether you sounded like a person or a hostage

Those four move in a fortnight. Grammar does not, and looking for it will make you conclude that nothing happened.

## Today's work (6 min)

**Record (2 min).** Same prompt as Day 1: **"Who I am."** 90 seconds. No preparation, no notes, one take. Save as `day-14-compare.m4a`.

**Listen — new first (1 min).** Play today's recording.

**Listen — old second (1 min).** Play `day-01-baseline.m4a`.

Order matters. Old-then-new makes you hear the old one as normal. New-then-old makes the gap obvious.

**Write the difference (2 min).** Three specific observations. Not "it's better" — specific:

- *"I started in 2 seconds. On Day 1 I took 7."*
- *"I never fully stopped. On Day 1 I stopped four times."*

If you genuinely hear no difference, write that too, and check your Week 1 and Week 2 hesitation averages instead. The numbers often move before the ear can hear it.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** today is solo by design.

**Optional — with a person:** play them both recordings **without saying which is which** and ask which speaker sounds more comfortable. They will pick correctly, and it will mean more than your own opinion.

## Record & review (2 min)

Score **today's recording** on these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did it move? |
| **Hesitation** | How much searching? |
| **Recovery** | What happened after a stumble? |

Then find the same three scores you gave the Day 1 file on Day 7. Compare directly.

**Average the week (1 min).** Add your seven hesitation scores from Days 8–14, divide by seven, and write it as **Week 2 average** next to Week 1's.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it.

## Stretch (optional)

Complete the weekly challenge if you have not: one full hour thinking in English. You have until tonight.
