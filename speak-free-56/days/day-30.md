# Day 30 — Hot Takes

**Week 5 · Opinions & Disagreement · ~20 minutes · Format: conviction monologue**

## Why today matters

You believe you sound uncertain because you *are* uncertain. Today disproves that.

You will defend something completely trivial — which sandwich is best, whether socks with sandals is defensible — with total conviction. The topic does not matter, and that is the design. When the content is worthless, the only thing left to practise is delivery.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud, with full conviction: *"Tuesday is the worst day of the week."*
2. Say it again, louder, with no hedging.
3. Say aloud: *"Conviction is how I say it, not how sure I am."*

## The concept (2 min)

Conviction is produced by four mechanical things, none of which require belief:

**No hedging at the start.** Open with the claim, not the qualification.
**Short sentences.** Long ones sound like negotiating with yourself.
**Drop your pitch at the end.** Rising pitch turns a statement into a question.
**Do not trail off.** Finish the sentence and stop. Do not add "…or whatever".

Do those four and you sound certain about anything, including things you made up thirty seconds ago.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Pick three trivial positions (2 min).** Genuinely unimportant. For example:

- The best time to eat dinner is 6pm.
- Cold weather is better than hot.
- Films are too long now.
- Tea is better than coffee.

**Take 1 (3 min).** 60 seconds defending position one, with total conviction. Claim first, two reasons, restate the claim. Save as `day-30-take-1.m4a`.

**Take 2 (3 min).** Position two. Same. Save as `day-30-take-2.m4a`.

**Take 3 (3 min).** Position three — but pick one you **actively disagree with**. Same conviction. Save as `day-30-take-3.m4a`.

**The rule: no hedging words at all.** Banned: *maybe, probably, I think, kind of, sort of, I don't know, perhaps.* If one slips out, finish the sentence and carry on — do not restart.

**Review (2 min).** Play Take 3. That is the one where you disagreed with yourself. If it sounds as convinced as the others, you have proved delivery is separable from belief.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** all three takes run alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** deliver all three and ask them to guess which one you do not actually believe. If they cannot, it worked.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 30 — Hedge Detector** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **Take 3** once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Expression** | Did you sound convinced? |
| **Clarity** | Was the position stated in the first sentence? |
| **Flow** | Did removing hedges make you faster or shakier? |

Flow often dips today. Hedging words are also stalling words, and taking them away removes a crutch you did not know you were using.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log how many hedge words escaped. Compare with Day 4's filler count — they are the same habit wearing a different coat.

## Stretch (optional)

Defend a `hard` position from the prompt bank with the same conviction. Serious topics make hedging much harder to resist.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you argue the other side properly — not as a game, but well enough that someone who holds that view would agree you had stated it fairly.
