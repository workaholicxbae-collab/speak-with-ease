# Day 38 — STAR Answers

**Week 6 · Professional English · ~22 minutes · Format: structured Q&A**

## Why today matters

"Tell me about a time you handled a difficult situation."

You start describing the situation. Then more context. Then background to the context. Two minutes later you have not reached what you actually did, and the interviewer has stopped listening.

STAR fixes the proportions.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud one work problem you solved. Thirty seconds, no structure. Notice where you spent the time.
2. Say it again in four sentences only.
3. Say aloud: *"They want the action, not the background."*

## The concept (2 min)

Four parts, in this order, with these proportions:

> **Situation** — one sentence. Where and when. *"Last year on a delivery project."*
> **Task** — one sentence. What needed to happen. *"We were three weeks behind."*
> **Action** — **three or four sentences.** What *you* did. Specifically you, not the team.
> **Result** — one sentence, with a number if you have one.

Most people spend eighty percent on Situation. STAR spends sixty percent on Action, because that is the only part being assessed.

The word "I" belongs in Action. "We" in that section is where good answers go to die.

## Today's work (14 min)

**Map one story (3 min).** Take a real work situation. Say the four parts aloud, one sentence each except Action. Do not record yet.

**Round 1 — Five questions (8 min).** Record all five in one file. Sixty to ninety seconds each, STAR every time:

1. Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem.
2. Describe a time you disagreed with a colleague.
3. Tell me about a mistake you made.
4. Describe a time you had to learn something quickly.
5. Tell me about a time you worked under pressure.

Save as `day-38-star.m4a`.

**The rules:** Situation gets one sentence only · Action must use "I", not "we" · every answer ends with a Result.

**Review (3 min).** Play it back. For each answer, mark how long you spent on Situation. Anything over fifteen seconds is too long.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** the five questions run alone.

**Upgrade — with a person:** have them ask the questions and cut you off at ninety seconds regardless of where you are. The interruption teaches proportion faster than any instruction.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 38 — STAR Interviewer** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **answers 3 and 5** only. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Were all four parts present, in order? |
| **Flow** | Did you reach Action without wandering? |
| **Recovery** | On question 3, did admitting a mistake destabilise you? |

Question 3 is deliberately included. Talking about your own failure in a second language is where composure usually breaks.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log how many answers ended with an actual Result. Most people drop it under time pressure, and it is the part that gets remembered.

## Stretch (optional)

Answer all five again using **different** stories. If you only have one story that fits everything, you will be caught out in a real interview.
