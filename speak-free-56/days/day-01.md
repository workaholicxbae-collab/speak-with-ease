# Day 1 — Baseline

**Week 1 · Break the Silence · ~20 minutes · Format: unedited monologue**

## Why today matters

You cannot measure a change you never took a starting reading of. Today you take that reading, and it will be uncomfortable, because it is honest.

This is the single most important recording in the program. You will hear it again on Days 7, 14, 28 and 56. Every piece of proof you get later depends on you being truthful now.

## Warm-up (3 min)

Stand up. Do not skip this because it looks silly.

1. Roll your shoulders back five times. Slowly.
2. Yawn on purpose, wide, three times. This opens the throat.
3. Hum one long note for five seconds. Repeat three times.
4. Say out loud, at normal volume: **"My name is ___ and I am recording this on ___."** Say it three times.

You have now spoken English out loud today. The hardest part is behind you.

## The concept (2 min)

A baseline is not a performance. It is a photograph.

Nobody photographs a room before decorating it because the room looks good. They photograph it so that in six months there is something to compare against. If you tidy the room first, the photograph is worthless.

The same applies here. A baseline you are pleased with is a baseline you edited, and an edited baseline destroys the only reliable evidence of progress you will ever have.

## Today's work (12 min)

**Record 90 seconds on the topic: "Who I am."**

1. Open your recorder. Do not write notes. Do not plan.
2. Start a 90-second timer. Press record.
3. Talk about yourself. Name, work, where you live, what your days look like, what you want from your English.
4. **The rule: you may repeat yourself. You may not stop.** If you run dry at second forty, say anything — describe the room, say "I don't know what to say next" — but keep sound coming out.
5. At 90 seconds, stop.

**Then:**

6. Save it as `day-01-baseline.m4a`.
7. Log it in [../trackers/recording-log.md](../trackers/recording-log.md).
8. **Do not listen to it.** Not today. You listen on Day 7.

If you stopped halfway and restarted, that is fine — keep the file anyway. Do not do a clean second take.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (use this today):** today is deliberately solo. No partner, no AI. The first recording should have no audience at all, so there is nothing to perform for.

## Record & review (3 min)

You have the recording. Today you do not review it — that is the whole design.

Instead, write one sentence in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md):

> *"What I expect to hear when I play this back on Day 7:"*

Be specific. Write what you fear. On Day 7 you will find out whether you were right, and most people discover they were harsher than the recording deserved.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?**

10 means constant searching. 1 means almost none. Most people land between 6 and 9 today. Log it. High is normal and expected — this is your ceiling reading, not your verdict.

## Stretch (optional)

Record a second 90 seconds on a different question: *"What do I want to be able to do in English that I cannot do today?"*

Save it as `day-01-goal.m4a`. Do not listen to it either. It becomes part of the Day 56 comparison.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you sound fluent. Not eventually — tomorrow. You will borrow the fluency from someone else, and it will feel like getting away with something.
