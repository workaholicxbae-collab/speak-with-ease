# Day 17 — The Follow-Up Game

**Week 3 · Everyday Conversation · ~21 minutes · Format: question-chain drill**

## Why today matters

Conversations die on you. Someone answers, and then there is a silence you have no idea how to fill, and you conclude your English failed.

Your English did not fail. You ran out of questions — which is a completely different problem with a completely fixable cause.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Say aloud: *"I went to Delhi last week."* Now ask three different follow-up questions about it.
2. Do it again with: *"I've started running."*
3. Say aloud: *"When I don't know what to say, I ask."*

## The concept (2 min)

Every answer contains its own next question. You do not have to invent one — you extract it.

*"I went to Delhi last week."* Inside that: **why** (work or holiday), **who** (alone or with someone), **how** (train or plane), **what** (what did you do), **when** (how long), **how was it** (opinion).

Six questions, none of which required creativity. The information was handed to you.

A conversation only dies when both people stop extracting.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Warm chain (3 min).** Take a statement — *"I moved house last year"* — and ask **five** follow-ups aloud. Do not answer them. Just generate.

**Chain 1 (4 min).** Pick any `medium` prompt from [../resources/prompt-bank.md](../resources/prompt-bank.md). Play both roles, recording:

1. Ask the question.
2. Answer it in two or three sentences.
3. **Ask a follow-up built from something in that answer.**
4. Answer that.
5. Follow up again. Answer again.

Three exchanges deep, minimum. Save as `day-17-chain-1.m4a`.

**Chain 2 (3 min).** New topic, four exchanges deep. Save as `day-17-chain-2.m4a`.

**The rule: each follow-up must contain a word from the previous answer.** This forces genuine listening instead of running down a list.

**Review (3 min).** Play Chain 2. Mark any question that could have been asked before the conversation started — those are the script questions, and they are the ones that make people feel unheard.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** both chains run alone, both roles.

**Upgrade — with a person:** ask them one real question and commit to four follow-ups before you say anything about yourself.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 17 — Chain Partner** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **Chain 2** once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did the chain move, or stall between exchanges? |
| **Clarity** | Were the questions easy to answer? |
| **Hesitation** | How long before each follow-up arrived? |

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log how deep your longest chain went. Four or more is strong.

## Stretch (optional)

Run a chain six exchanges deep on a topic you find boring. Extracting questions from dull material is the real skill — interesting topics ask their own questions.
