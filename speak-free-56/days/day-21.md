# Day 21 — Rally Test

**Week 3 · Everyday Conversation · ~12 minutes · Format: recorded conversation and reflection**

## Why today matters

Six days ago you had eight openers and no reliable way to keep a conversation moving. Today you hold three minutes of unscripted back-and-forth and record it.

This is a lighter day. One conversation, one listen, one reflection — but it is the first recording in the program with another voice in it.

## Warm-up (2 min)

No vocal warm-up. Instead:

Write one line in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md): **the moment in a conversation you still dread most.** Opening it? The silence after an answer? Being asked something unexpected?

Name it before you start, then notice whether it actually happens.

## The concept (2 min)

You now have every piece: openers from Day 15, follow-ups from Day 17, confirmation from Day 18, reactions from Day 20.

Today you use them together, unscripted, which is the only test that means anything. Individual drills prove a move exists. A rally proves you can reach for it while something else is happening.

## Today's work (6 min)

**Set up (1 min).** Choose one:

- **With a person** — ask for three minutes and press record. Tell them it is for a course; you do not need to hide it today.
- **With AI voice mode** — use the **Day 21 — Rally Partner** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).
- **Solo fallback** — both roles yourself, three minutes, switching clearly.

**The conversation (3 min).** Three minutes. Any topic.

**The rules:** no script · no switching languages · if it stalls, ask a follow-up rather than waiting.

Save as `day-21-rally.m4a`.

**Listen (2 min).** Play it back once. Count two things:

- **Questions you asked.** Three or more in three minutes is a rally. Fewer is an interview where you were the one being examined.
- **Silences over three seconds.** These are where the moves have not become automatic yet.

## Interactive layer

Chosen above — with a person, with AI, or solo. All three are valid today.

## Record & review (2 min)

Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Did the exchange move, or lurch between turns? |
| **Recovery** | When it stalled, did you restart it? |
| **Expression** | Did you sound like a person in a conversation? |

**Average the week (1 min).** Add your hesitation scores from Days 15–21, divide by seven, and log it as **Week 3 average** beside Weeks 1 and 2.

Three weeks is enough to see a line rather than two points. Look at the shape.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it.

Then check the fear you named in the warm-up. Did it happen? Most people find the thing they dread most did not occur at all.

## Stretch (optional)

Run the rally again with someone who talks fast, or on a topic you know nothing about. Both remove your ability to prepare.

## Tomorrow

Week 4. You stop trading information and start telling stories — and find out that being interesting takes less English than being correct.
