# Day 33 — Five Rounds

**Week 5 · Opinions & Disagreement · ~22 minutes · Format: live debate rounds**

## Why today matters

Stating an opinion once is easy. Holding it after someone has questioned it three times is a different skill entirely — and it is the one that gets noticed.

Today you go five exchanges deep on a single position without retreating.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. State any opinion aloud. Now say the strongest objection to it. Now answer that objection.
2. Do it again with a different opinion.
3. Say aloud: *"Answering an objection is not the same as losing."*

## The concept (2 min)

Under pressure there are three exits, and two of them are surrender.

**Collapse** — *"Yeah, you're right, forget it."* You did not change your mind; you left.
**Vagueness** — *"Well, it's complicated, there are many factors."* A retreat wearing a disguise.
**Hold** — you answer the objection and restate your position, possibly narrower than before.

Only the third is available today. Narrowing your position under a good objection is legitimate and intelligent. Abandoning it because the conversation became uncomfortable is not.

## Today's work (14 min)

**Choose your position (1 min).** Something you actually believe. Any `medium` or `hard` prompt from [../resources/prompt-bank.md](../resources/prompt-bank.md).

**Set up (1 min).** Choose one:

- **With AI** — use the **Day 33 — Five Rounds Opponent** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).
- **With a person** — ask them to disagree with everything you say for five exchanges.
- **Solo fallback** — both roles yourself. Play the opponent genuinely, using your Day 31 steelman skill.

**The debate (9 min).** Record the whole thing.

1. State your position in one sentence.
2. They object. You answer, then restate.
3. Repeat until five full exchanges are done.

Save as `day-33-five-rounds.m4a`.

**The rules:** never say "you're right, I give up" · never retreat into "it's complicated" · you may narrow your position, but you must say so out loud.

**Review (3 min).** Play it back. Find the round where you were closest to collapsing. That is your pressure limit, and it is worth knowing the number.

## Interactive layer

Chosen above — AI, person, or solo. All three work today.

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **rounds 4 and 5** only. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Recovery** | After a strong objection, did you answer or drift? |
| **Clarity** | Was your position still identifiable at round five? |
| **Flow** | Did sustained pressure break your speaking? |

Scoring the late rounds is deliberate. Everyone is fine at round one.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log **which round was hardest**. Expect a drop in your score today — this is the hardest day of Week 5, and a lower number means the difficulty was right.

## Stretch (optional)

Eight rounds instead of five, on a position you hold weakly. Sustained pressure on shaky ground is the real test.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you are allowed to say "it depends" — but only if you can say precisely what it depends on.
