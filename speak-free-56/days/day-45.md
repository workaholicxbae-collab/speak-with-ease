# Day 45 — Cut Off

**Week 7 · Pressure & Spontaneity · ~21 minutes · Format: interruption recovery drill**

## Why today matters

Someone interrupts you and your sentence evaporates. You either surrender the floor or restart from the beginning — and restarting is the tell that signals, to everyone in the room, that you were not confident.

Today you practise being interrupted deliberately, so that continuing becomes the reflex instead.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Start describing your day. Stop yourself abruptly mid-sentence. Now continue from exactly where you stopped, not from the start.
2. Do it twice more. Different sentence each time.
3. Say aloud: *"I don't go back. I go on."*

## The concept (2 min)

An interruption gives you three choices. Two of them cost you the room.

**Surrender** — you stop and never return to your point. It disappears.
**Restart** — you go back to the beginning of your sentence. This reads as being flustered, and it wastes the group's patience.
**Hold and continue** — you either finish your thought or take it back afterwards.

Two phrases do almost all the work:

> *"Let me just finish this point —"*
> *"Coming back to what I was saying —"*

Neither is rude. Both are used constantly by people who are listened to.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Drill the phrases (2 min).** Say both, five times each, calmly. Not defensively — the tone matters more than the words.

**Round 1 — Self-interrupt (4 min).** Speak on any `medium` prompt for two minutes. **Every 20 seconds, interrupt yourself out loud** — say "wait, but actually" or clap once — then continue the original sentence from where you were.

Record. Save as `day-45-self-interrupt.m4a`.

**Round 2 — Hostile interruption (4 min).** Both roles. You speak; the other role interrupts four times with objections and changes of subject. Each time, use one of the two phrases and return to your point. Save as `day-45-hostile.m4a`.

**The rule: never restart a sentence.** Continue it, or explicitly park it and come back. Restarting counts as a failed round.

**Round 3 — Interrupted at the worst moment (3 min).** Same, but the interruptions land mid-word. Save as `day-45-midword.m4a`.

## Interactive layer

**Solo fallback (default):** all three rounds run alone, both roles.

**Upgrade — with a person:** ask them to interrupt you rudely and often. Tell them explicitly not to be polite about it, or they will be.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 45 — Interrupter** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to **Round 2** once. Score these three — see [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md):

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Recovery** | Did you continue, or restart? |
| **Expression** | Did you sound steady or rattled? |
| **Clarity** | Was your original point still delivered? |

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

Also log how many times you restarted instead of continuing. That count should be zero by Day 56.

## Stretch (optional)

Be interrupted eight times in two minutes. At that density you stop treating each interruption as an event and start treating it as noise, which is the correct response.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you speak while your body is busy — walking, standing, with noise around you.
