# Day 5 — Send It

**Week 1 · Break the Silence · ~20 minutes · Format: voice note to a real person**

## Why today matters

Everything so far has been private. You could delete it. Nobody was listening.

Today someone listens. That changes the physical sensation of speaking completely — and it is the sensation you actually need to train, because no real conversation happens in a room by yourself.

This is the day people skip. Do not skip it.

## Warm-up (3 min)

1. Shoulders back, five rolls.
2. Say aloud, three times: *"They are not marking me."*
3. Say your opening line out loud once, exactly as you will say it: *"Hey — I'm doing an English speaking program, so I'm sending this in English."*

That sentence does most of the work. It reframes the message from *showing off* to *doing an exercise*, and it releases you from sounding perfect.

## The concept (2 min)

A voice note is the safest possible real audience.

No eye contact. No live pressure. Nobody waiting while you think.

But it is still a real person who will really hear it — so the fear is genuine, just turned down to about twenty percent. That is the right dose for Day 5.

## Today's work (13 min)

**Choose your person (2 min).** Someone who will not mock you. Warmth matters more than their English level.

**Draft nothing.** No script, no bullet points. Thirty seconds of thinking, then record.

**Record and send (6 min).**

1. Open your messaging app, not your recorder.
2. Press record. Open with the line from the warm-up.
3. Talk for **60 seconds** on one of: your week · something that annoyed you · a plan you are making.
4. **The rule: two re-records maximum.** If the third take is not good enough, send the third take anyway.
5. **Send it.** Today is not complete until the message has left your phone.

**Save a copy (2 min).** Record the same 60 seconds into your own recorder as `day-05-voicenote.m4a`.

**Sit with it (3 min).** Do not refresh for a reply. Notice what your body did when you pressed send, and write one line about it in [../trackers/progress-tracker.md](../trackers/progress-tracker.md).

## Interactive layer

**With a person (default today):** the voice note above.

**Solo fallback — if you genuinely have nobody:** send the voice note to yourself, then listen twelve hours later as though it arrived from someone else. This is weaker. Use it once, not as your habit.

**Upgrade — with AI:** use the **Day 5 — Warm Friend** prompt in [../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](../resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

## Record & review (3 min)

Listen to `day-05-voicenote.m4a` once. Score these three:

| Dimension | Score 1–5 |
|---|---|
| **Expression** | Did you sound like a person talking to a friend? |
| **Flow** | Did it move, or did you keep resetting? |
| **Clarity** | Would they have understood without effort? |

See [../resources/rubric.md](../resources/rubric.md).

Expression is the one to watch. Most people flatten their voice the moment there is an audience. If you sounded warmer here than on Day 3, that is a real result.

## Confidence checkpoint

**Rate 1–10: how much did you hesitate today?** Log it.

Also log **how many takes it took**. That number is a direct measure of self-trust, and it should fall over the next seven weeks.

## Stretch (optional)

Send a second voice note to a different person, on a different topic, with a hard limit of **one take**. Press record, speak, send. No listening back before it goes.

## Tomorrow

Tomorrow you stop sounding like one person. Same words, three completely different deliveries — and you find out your voice has settings you have never used in English.
