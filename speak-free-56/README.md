# Speak Free — 56 Days to English Confidence

You can read English. You can follow a film. You sit in meetings and understand every word.

Then someone turns to you and asks a question, and your mouth stops working.

This program is for that gap and nothing else.

## What this is not

Not a grammar course. Not vocabulary lists. Not an app that gives you a streak and a cartoon owl. You do not have a knowledge problem. You have an output problem, and output only improves by producing output.

## What this is

Eight weeks. Fifty-six days. Twenty to twenty-five minutes a day.

Every day you speak out loud and produce something — a recording, a voice note, a real conversation. No day ends with "read this and think about it."

| Week | Theme | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Break the Silence | You speak before you feel ready |
| 2 | Think in English | You stop translating in your head |
| 3 | Everyday Conversation | You return the ball instead of performing |
| 4 | Storytelling & Personality | You become interesting, not just correct |
| 5 | Opinions & Disagreement | You hold a position under mild pressure |
| 6 | Professional English | You are clear at work under time limits |
| 7 | Pressure & Spontaneity | You recover instead of freezing |
| 8 | Own the Room | You deliver to a real audience |

## The engine

On Day 1 you record ninety seconds. It will be uncomfortable. You save it and you do not delete it.

You hear it again on Days 7, 14, 28 and 56.

That recording is the entire emotional spine of this program. Most people cannot feel their own progress while it happens — the change is too gradual, and you are the worst-placed person to notice it. The Day 1 file solves that. It is proof you cannot argue with.

## How to start

1. Read [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md). It takes five minutes and sets up your recording.
2. Skim [CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md) so you know the shape of the eight weeks.
3. Open [days/day-01.md](days/day-01.md) and do it today. Not tomorrow.

## The rules of the game

- **Speaking beats accuracy.** For the first four weeks, a fluent wrong sentence beats a correct silence. Every time.
- **You may repeat yourself. You may not stop.** This appears on almost every day. It is the whole method in one line.
- **Do not re-record to sound better.** Re-record only when a day explicitly tells you to.
- **Missing a day is not failure.** Missing a day and then quitting is. See [FAQ.md](FAQ.md).

## What you need

A phone with a voice recorder. A door you can close. That is the full list.

A conversation partner or an AI voice mode makes some days richer, but **every day in this program runs solo.** Days that involve another person always carry a solo fallback that works just as well.
