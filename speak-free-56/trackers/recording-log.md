# Recording Log

Every recording you make, and where it lives.

This file looks like admin. It is not. On Day 56 you play Day 1 and Day 56 back to back, and that comparison is the payoff for the whole eight weeks. If you cannot find the Day 1 file, the payoff does not happen.

**Two digits, always.** `day-07`, not `day-7`. Otherwise Day 10 sorts above Day 7 and by Week 5 the folder is unusable.

## If you lose the Day 1 recording

It happens. Phones die.

Do **not** record a replacement and label it Day 1 — a baseline recorded in Week 3 is not a baseline, and quietly pretending otherwise removes the one honest measurement in the program.

Instead: record a fresh 90 seconds today on the same prompt, label it `day-XX-restart-baseline.m4a`, and compare against that from now on. A shorter honest comparison beats a longer dishonest one.
