# Progress Tracker

One row a day. Two minutes, no more. Fill it in immediately after the session — later never happens.

**Hesitation** is the confidence checkpoint score from that day's file: **1 = almost none, 10 = constant searching.** Higher is worse.

**Note** is one line. What you noticed, not what you wish had happened.
