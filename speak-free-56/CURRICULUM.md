# The Full Map — 56 Days

Eight weeks. One concept each. Twenty to twenty-five minutes a day, except the checkpoint days, which are twelve.

Days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49 and 56 are deliberately light. They are reflection and replay. You are not being let off — recovery is part of the design, and a program with no lighter days is a program you quit in week three.

---

## Week 1 — Break the Silence
> Your mouth moves before your brain approves. Output beats accuracy.

| Day | Session | What you produce |
|---|---|---|
| [1](days/day-01.md) | **Baseline** — 90 seconds, unedited, "who I am" | The recording the whole program is measured against |
| [2](days/day-02.md) | **Borrowed Fluency** — shadow a clip, 3 passes | You hear yourself sound fluent, on loan |
| [3](days/day-03.md) | **The 60-Second Rule** — one ordinary object | Sixty unbroken seconds |
| [4](days/day-04.md) | **Kill the Um** — silence instead of filler | A filler count, twice |
| [5](days/day-05.md) | **Send It** — a voice note to a real person | A message actually sent |
| [6](days/day-06.md) | **Three Moods** — angry, excited, bored | Same words, three deliveries |
| [7](days/day-07.md) | **Replay** — listen to Day 1 | 3 wins, 1 focus, week average |

**Weekly challenge:** speak English out loud to one human being who is not in your household.

## How the pieces connect

- **[resources/rubric.md](resources/rubric.md)** — five dimensions: Flow, Hesitation, Clarity, Expression, Recovery. Every day scores exactly three of them, never all five. Scoring everything means noticing nothing.
- **[resources/prompt-bank.md](resources/prompt-bank.md)** — 200 prompts tagged `easy`, `medium`, `hard`. Days point you at a tag, not a fixed prompt, so you can re-run any day with fresh material.
- **[trackers/progress-tracker.md](trackers/progress-tracker.md)** — one row per day. Hesitation score 1–10 and one line of note. Two minutes total.
- **[trackers/recording-log.md](trackers/recording-log.md)** — where each file lives. Without this, the Day 56 comparison does not happen.

## The four replay points

Days **7, 14, 28 and 56** send you back to the Day 1 recording.

This is the only reliable way to feel improvement while you are inside it. You cannot perceive your own gradual change — you need a fixed reference point, and Day 1 is it.
