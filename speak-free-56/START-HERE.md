# Start Here — Five Minutes

Do this now, before Day 1. It removes every excuse you might use tomorrow.

---

## 1. Pick your recorder (1 min)

Open the voice recorder already on your phone. That is it. That is the whole decision.

Do not download a new app. Do not compare apps. Do not buy a microphone. People who spend Day 1 choosing recording software are avoiding the recording.

**Test it now:** record five seconds, play it back, confirm you can hear yourself.

## 2. Make the folder (1 min)

Create one folder. Name it `Speak Free`.

Inside it, save every recording with this exact pattern:

```
day-01-baseline.m4a
day-02-shadowing.m4a
day-03-object.m4a
```

Two digits for the day. Always. `day-07`, not `day-7` — otherwise Day 10 sorts above Day 7 and by Week 5 your folder is chaos.

Log each file in [trackers/recording-log.md](trackers/recording-log.md) as you go.

## 3. Find your door (1 min)

You need one place where you can talk out loud without an audience. A room, a car, a stairwell, a walk. Decide now which one it is and when you can be there.

If someone in your home will overhear you, tell them today what you are doing. Say it plainly: *"I'm doing a speaking program for eight weeks. You'll hear me talking to myself. Ignore it."* Ten seconds of awkwardness now prevents fifty-six days of whispering.

## 4. Set the time (1 min)

Pick a slot and put it in your calendar for the next seven days. Twenty-five minutes.

Morning is better than evening. Not because of science — because evening you will negotiate, and morning you will not have had the chance yet.

## 5. Read the rules (1 min)

These four lines govern all fifty-six days.

> **You may repeat yourself. You may not stop.**
> Running out of words is fine. Going silent to search for a better word is the habit we are killing.

> **Do not delete recordings.**
> Especially the bad ones. Especially Day 1. The bad ones are the evidence.

> **Do not re-record to sound better.**
> Only re-record when a day tells you to. A polished second take teaches you nothing about how you actually speak.

> **Ugly and finished beats beautiful and abandoned.**
> Every day, the target is *done*, not *good*.

---

## The three things people get wrong in week one

**They judge the Day 1 recording.** It is supposed to be bad. That is the point of a baseline. A baseline you are happy with is a baseline that was not honest.

**They keep restarting.** You stumble at second eleven, you stop, you begin again. Do that and you spend fifty-six days practising the first eleven seconds. Speak through it.

**They wait until they feel ready.** You will not feel ready on Day 1, or Day 20, or Day 56. Readiness is not the entry fee. Twenty-two minutes is.

## Where to find clips

Twenty of the fifty-six days ask you to listen to something. [resources/video-library.md](resources/video-library.md) has channels and search links for each of them.

It gives you searches rather than fixed videos on purpose: videos get deleted, and several days want new material every time you repeat them.

## Optional: an AI voice partner

Some days offer a roleplay. Any AI with a voice mode works. Paste-ready prompts live in [resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md](resources/ai-roleplay-prompts.md).

Every one of those prompts tells the AI three things: stay in character, do not correct your grammar mid-conversation, and give feedback only at the end. That last instruction matters more than it looks — mid-conversation correction is exactly what taught you to freeze.

**This is optional.** Every roleplay day has a solo fallback that works.
