# Prompt Bank — 200 Prompts

Tagged `easy`, `medium`, `hard`. Days point you at a **tag**, not a fixed prompt — so you can re-run any day with new material and it stays a real exercise.

**How to pick:** close your eyes and stab at the list, or use the last two digits of the current time. Do not read three and choose the comfortable one. Choosing is preparation, and preparation is what we are removing.

| Tag | What it means | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| `easy` | Concrete. Visible. Personal. No position required. | Weeks 1–2, or any day you are tired |
| `medium` | Narrative or opinion. Some abstraction. | Weeks 3–5 |
| `hard` | Abstract, hypothetical, or adversarial. | Weeks 6–8, or Stretch |

## Re-running a day

Any day that names a tag can be re-run. Take the same instructions, pick a different prompt at the same tag, and go. This is how you repeat Day 3 or Day 43 without repeating yourself.

If a day feels too easy, take a prompt one tag harder. If you froze completely, take one tag easier and run the same day again tomorrow. Neither is cheating — the tag is a dial, and you are the one holding it.
