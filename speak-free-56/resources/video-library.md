# Video Library

What to watch, for the days that need something to listen to.

---

## Why this is not a list of 56 links

Two reasons, both practical.

**Fixed links rot.** Videos get deleted, made private, or blocked in your country. A list of 56 specific clips would be part-broken within a year, and you would hit the dead one on the morning you were ready to work.

**You are meant to re-run days.** Day 2, Day 11 and Day 24 all want *new* material each time. A single fixed clip defeats the exercise the second time you do it.

So this file gives you two things per need: **channels** worth returning to, and a **search link** that always works and always returns something current. Pick the first result that fits. Do not browse — browsing is preparation, and preparation is what this program removes.

| **Rachel's English** | Rhythm, stress and connected speech — best for shadowing weeks | [youtube.com/@rachelsenglish](https://www.youtube.com/@rachelsenglish) |
| **English with Lucy** | Pronunciation and phrasing, clearly structured | [youtube.com/@EnglishwithLucy](https://www.youtube.com/@EnglishwithLucy) |
| **Learn English With Lucy** | Calm, slower podcast-style listening | [youtube.com/@LearnEnglishWithLucy](https://www.youtube.com/@LearnEnglishWithLucy) |
| **TED** | Structure, delivery and pacing — Weeks 7 and 8 | [Talks to watch before an interview](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOGi5-fAu8bHaEGV52hSzl8QeUM4933Xy) |

**One rule when choosing a clip: never use a news reader.** Their rhythm is artificial — evenly paced, no hesitation, no real pauses — and copying it teaches you to sound like a broadcast rather than a person. Interviews, podcasts and street conversations are what you want.

## By need

### Shadowing — Days 2, 24

One person, talking naturally, clear enough to follow without subtitles. Two minutes is plenty.

- [Search: english shadowing practice conversation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=english+shadowing+practice+conversation)
- [Search: slow english conversation shadowing](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=slow+english+conversation+shadowing+practice)
- Channels: **Rachel's English**, **BBC Learning English**

### Muted video for narration — Day 11

You will mute this, so the words do not matter. What matters is that **things keep happening** and the camera does not cut every two seconds.

Cooking, repairs, street scenes and sport all work. Avoid anything with fast editing.

- [Search: street walking tour 4k](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=street+walking+tour+4k)
- [Search: cooking no talking silent recipe](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cooking+no+talking+silent+recipe)
- [Search: how it's made process video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+its+made+process+video)

## If a link is dead

The channel links may change; the search links will not. Search links contain only a query, so they resolve against whatever is on YouTube today rather than against a video that existed when this was written.

If a channel has moved, search its name. If a search returns nothing useful, take the second page — do not spend the session choosing.
